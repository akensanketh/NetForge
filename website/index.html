TRANSPARENT PIPELINE

How NETFORGE Works

A streamlined 3-step cryptographic architecture that protects your packets from origin to destination.

STEP 01

Client Configuration & Profile

Your client configures an authenticated VLESS identity with your dedicated UUID and ephemeral Reality keys. No user account credentials are leaked over the wire.

STEP 02

Reality Stealth Handshake

The tunnel encapsulates packet flows inside standard TLS 1.3 handshakes targeting legitimate public domains (like Zoom and Google). Deep-packet firewalls cannot distinguish it from everyday web traffic.

STEP 03

High-Throughput Gateway Egress

Traffic routes directly through dedicated high-speed cloud nodes with native IPv4/IPv6 support, stripping ISP video throttles and masking your true origin IP address.
